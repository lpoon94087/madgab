Word Generator

Words for Charades, Pictionary, Password and any other guessing game. Play on one device, or add viewer screens that show each word.

How to play

Just words — pick a list and tap Next word whenever you need one. Words don't repeat until the list runs out.

Timed rounds — teams take turns. The player up holds the phone, gets their team to guess as many words as they can before the clock runs out, and taps Got It or Skip.

  • Charades — act it out, no talking.
  • Pictionary — draw it, no letters or numbers.
  • Password — one-word clues only.
  • Describe it — talk around it without saying any part of it.

More screens — this device is the moderator: it has every control. Other devices can scan the QR code under Show the words on other screens and become viewers, which show the current word, the clock and the scores, but can't change anything.
